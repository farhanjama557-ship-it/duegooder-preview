Detector available

Detection only. Section collection is not implemented yet.

Description

Targets Banner 9 self-service registration (StudentRegistrationSsb). The detector looks for public fingerprints on a university's own registration pages; it does not collect course sections yet.

Fingerprints the detector looks for

  • StudentRegistrationSsb
  • /ssb/term/termSelection
  • /ssb/searchResults
  • /ssb/sectionDetail
