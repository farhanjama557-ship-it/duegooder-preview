Detection Method

Implemented in lib/detectors/banner.js. Banner detection combines public URL patterns, page content, Ellucian/Banner HTML fingerprints and known Banner 9 SSB routes. Several independent signals are required: the word “banner” on a page is deliberately worth almost nothing, and a URL the detector constructed itself is never counted as evidence.

    Detection Process

    1Fetch the institution homepage (1 request)
    2Scan HTML for links that look like Banner self-service
    3Fetch the best candidate registration page, or try two well-known host patterns
    4Score matched signals by weight (URLs we constructed ourselves are not evidence)
    5Require at least one structural signal before classifying
    6Store the result and reuse it for 24h
