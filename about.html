About DueGooder

DueGooder is built for the KYX 2026 Hackathon to solve a real problem: course data is locked in hundreds of different university systems.

We build one connector per registration platform — not one scraper per school — to collect, normalize, and maintain public course sections at scale.

The problem

Universities publish course schedules in different formats across Banner, PeopleSoft, Workday, and many other systems. That makes them hard to access, compare, and build on.

Our solution

DueGooder detects the registration system, reuses a connector built for that platform, and normalizes every section into one consistent format with provenance and refresh tracking.

The impact

Easier access to course data enables better tools for students, advisors, and developers, and reduces the effort of rebuilding the same integration over and over.
